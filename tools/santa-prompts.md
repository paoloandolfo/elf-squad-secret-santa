# Santa portrait prompts

How to use (ChatGPT, Gemini or any image tool that accepts a photo):

1. Start one chat and do all 10 in it, so the style stays the same.
2. For each friend: upload their photo, paste the **shared style** and then their **personal line**.
3. Download each result as a square image and name it after the friend (`paolo.png`, `gigi.png`, …).
4. Send the 10 images back to Claude, which puts them in the app and the teaser.

Only upload photos of friends who are OK with it: the image tool's company receives them.

---

## Shared style (paste this every time)

> Turn the person in this photo into a cute, friendly Santa Claus character in the style of a modern 3D animated film. Keep them clearly recognisable: same face shape, eyes, eyebrows, hair colour and hairstyle, facial hair and skin tone. Head and shoulders only, facing the camera, centred, with space around the head so it fits inside a circle. They wear a Santa hat with white fur trim and a Santa coat with a white fur collar. Soft warm studio lighting, slightly rosy cheeks, a happy expression, a few soft snowflakes. Plain solid background in the colour given below. No text, no logos, no other people. Square image.
